about=Map Tips & Actions turns the plain hover tooltip into an information card. A docked designer builds cards from ready layouts (KPI cards, comparison bars, multi-metric donut charts, photo and media cards, raster readouts) and adapts to light, dark and high-contrast themes. Charts are drawn as SVG inside the card, so they display in QGIS's own tooltip.
    The action hub adds 22 ready URL and PyQGIS actions in five groups, from opening a location in a web map to copying geometry as GeoJSON or WKT, with an editor, token inserter and test runner. A live canvas readout samples raster pixels and stacked layers as you hover. Tips and actions are stored in the layer style, so colleagues see them even without the plugin.
    Manual: https://geophilo.com/qgis_maptips/
